## 2. FMECA Methodology & Criticality Scoring Standards
- **Governing Standards:** Executed per `MIL-STD-1629A` Method 101 (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) and Method 102 (Criticality Analysis), harmonized with `MIL-STD-882E` Tasks 202/205/206, `SAE ARP4754A` §5.1, `SAE ARP4761` §3.1-3.3, `NATO STANAG 4187` Ed. 5, `MIL-STD-331D`, `MIL-STD-461G`, `MIL-STD-810H`, `RTCA DO-178C / DO-254`, `RTCA DO-362A`, `ASTM F3269-17`, `JARUS SORA v2.5`, `NIST SP 800-82r3`, and `DoD Directive 3000.09` (`docs/research/RESEARCH_INVENTORY.md`).
- **4 Universal Failure Dimensions:** Every AST `part def` evaluates at least one distinct failure mode across each of the four universal dimensions:
  1. `Interface`: Connector discontinuities, harness opens/shorts, RF coax/link drops, serial/CAN/Ethernet bus framing or CRC faults, or mechanical coupling mismatches (`IF-MECH-001`, `CONN_01..24`).
  2. `State`: Out-of-sequence safety interlock transitions (`RBF #1 -> #2 -> #3`), finite-state-machine desynchronization (`UavFlightLifecycleState`, `EsadSafeArmState`, `Pl40LauncherState`, `RtaEnvelopeMonitorState`, `PaceC2LinkState`), or invalid UI/latch states.
  3. `Action`: Omission, premature execution, saturation, or clamping failure in active control, computation, power switching, firing, or scrub actions (`action def`s).
  4. `Resource`: DC rail voltage sag/brownout (`14.0 V`, `8.0 V`, `12.0 V`, `36.0 V`, `14.5 V` UVLO), energy exhaustion (`5400.0 s` / `0.20` Bingo), thermal/environmental saturation (`-20.0 to +50.0 degC`), CPU/bus scheduling contention (`GAP-WCET`), mechanical wearout (`100`-launch interval), or uncharacterized reliability margins (`GAP-REL`).
- **Risk Priority Number (`RPN = S * O * D`):** All ratings are strict integers with `S in [1, 10]`, `O in [1, 10]`, `D in [1, 10]`, and `RPN` equals the exact integer product `S * O * D`.

| Score | Severity (`S`) Criteria (`MIL-STD-882E` / `REQ_L_01..04`) | Occurrence (`O`) Criteria (Field / Sortie Likelihood & Gap Exposure) | Detection (`D`) Criteria (Automated Interlock / BIT Visibility Prior to Effect) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 - 2** | Negligible impact; no degradation to flight, C2, or ordnance safety | Extremely unlikely / remote under dual hardware-gated barriers | Certain automated hardware/software interlock blocks hazard prior to effect |
| **3 - 4** | Minor operational degradation or ground delay without airframe or crew hazard | Low occurrence; requires single procedural slip or connector fault | High detectability via pre-flight BIT (`0x11 PBIT`, pitot/servo sweep) or `CAN 1/2` / `RS-485` watchdog |
| **5 - 6** | Major mission abort, forced `RTL` (`REQ_L_03`), or controlled loss of payload effectiveness | Moderate occurrence under `GAP-01..07` boundary exposure or environmental stress (`-20..+50 degC`) | Moderate detectability; depends on secondary telemetry residual, operator prompt, or procedural checklist |
| **7 - 8** | Hazardous / severe mission failure: forced crash risk (`REQ_L_02`), common-cause C2/nav loss (`REQ_L_03`), or latent ordnance fault | Elevated occurrence where design margin is zero (`0.0 V` `GAP-01`) or uncharacterized (`GAP-ENV`/`GAP-WCET`) | Low detectability; lacks dedicated sensor/BIT for root cause (e.g., mechanical fastener torque, ESD touch, unprinted cutoff) |
| **9 - 10** | Catastrophic: crew fatality/injury (`REQ_L_01`), unintended detonation (`REQ_L_04`), or uncontrolled hull/control loss (`REQ_L_02`) | High likelihood of occurrence per sortie if procedural/parameter error occurs (`GAP-01`, `GAP-03`, `GAP-05..07`) | Very low / undetected until hazardous state manifests in flight or on armed rail |

- **Basis Tag Semantics:**
  - `SSOT`: Failure mode, threshold, or interlock is explicitly specified in `.pipeline/schema.sysml` or extracted OEM baseline artifacts (`schema/extracted/*`, `EXT-01`, `EXT-02`).
  - `Derived`: Failure mode or consequence is deduced analytically from hardware/bus coupling, common-cause dependencies, or explicit unprinted OEM gap boundaries (`GAP-01..07`, `GAP-WCET`, `GAP-REL`, `GAP-SEPDIST`, `GAP-ENV`, `GAP-SCRUB`).

### Component: MainBatteryAndFuseUnit (BOM-AV-002, BOM-AV-003)

- **Hardware, Mass, Power & Bus Provenance**: `MainBatteryAndFuseUnit` combines the removable `12S2P Li metal BEI` primary pack (`maximus20` cells, matched by QR/ID to fuselage, `BOM-AV-002`) with the inline `200.0 A` DC automotive overcurrent fuse (`BOM-AV-003`, `PRM-AV-005`). It sources the unregulated `36.0-50.4 V` DC main bus (`PRM-AV-001`..`PRM-AV-002`) with a `49.0-50.0 V` pre-flight check window (`PRM-AV-003`..`PRM-AV-004`) across `IF-PWR-001` (red `Batt Power` harness to `200.0 A` fuse) and `IF-PWR-002` (fuse to `PowerMeterAndRbfSwitch.mainDcIn` via `CONN_01`: `batteryAndFuse.protectedDcOut` -> `powerMeter.mainDcIn`). System endurance is up to `90.0 min` (`5400.0 s`, `PRM-PERF-001`..`PRM-PERF-002`) with a derived `0.20` (`1080.0 s`) statutory Bingo energy reserve (`PRM-PERF-003`) over the `-20.0 to +50.0 degC` airframe ambient envelope (`PRM-PERF-018`..`PRM-PERF-019`); standalone pack mass (`kg`) is unprinted in OEM baseline docs (total airframe MTOW is `17.0 kg`, `PRM-AF-008`).
- **Architectural & Gap Provenance**: Software-resident Simplex RTA executes on `FlightControllerCubeOrangePlus` (monitoring `bus < 36.0 V` and `Bingo <= 0.20` via `PowerMeterAndRbfSwitch` on `CAN 1`; zero standalone RTA hardware box). Zero electrical or CAN wires cross `PL-40 <-> UAV` (`IF-MECH-001`), and the `2x 50.0 V` EPS recovery igniter rails are non-production `Optional RnD` (`Recovery System: No`). Explicit applicable gaps: `GAP-01` (upstream main bus droop/loss collapses `EPS` `14.0 V` `ESAD` rail with `0.0 V` brownout margin), `GAP-05` (`49.0-50.0 V` `12S2P` baseline rejects `23.9-25.0 V` legacy UI screenshots), `GAP-06` (`RBF #2` main bus energization must precede `RBF #3` pull), `GAP-ENV` (pack energy `Wh`/`Ah`, `SoC` floor, pack mass, and ESC/motor current/thermal cutoffs unprinted), and `GAP-REL` (piece-part `lambda`/MTBF unprinted).
