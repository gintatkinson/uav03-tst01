# Cited Research Inventory and Normative Baseline: Avenger 5 V2.5 UAS

## 1. Scope & System Identification
- **System Identifier:** `Avenger 5 V2.5 UAS (ASTK A5 V2.5 / ELI PL-40 Rev 2.6 / Artron ESAD / SwarmC2)`
- **Operational Domain:** `Tactical Loitering Munition Uncrewed Aircraft System (17.0 kg MTOW X-wing pusher airframe, pneumatic rail catapult launch, EO/IR terminal guidance, STANAG 4187 ordnance safe and arm control)`
- **Research Scope:** Grounded normative baseline and clause-level allocation governing lifecycle systems engineering, requirements traceability, ConOps and Tactical Mission Intent synthesis, system/software/hardware safety assurance, STANAG 4187 ordnance arming interlocks, EMI/EMC and environmental qualification, 4.4-5.0 GHz C2 link continuity, software-hosted Simplex Run-Time Assurance (RTA) inside `Cube orange+`, OT/C2 cryptographic access control, human-in-the-loop (HITL) weapon control, and OEM hardware/parametric baselines (`schema/extracted/oem_bom.md`, `schema/extracted/parametric_limits.md`, `.pipeline/schema.sysml`).
- **Applicability Statement:** Governs all downstream engineering specification deliverables for `Avenger5V25IntegratedSystem` (`15` SysML `part def` assemblies, `24` `connection def` interfaces, `46` `requirement def`s, `19` `action def`s, `18` `constraint def`s, `8` `use case def`s, `8` `interaction def`s, `8` `test case def`s, and `5` `state def`s). Production `Avenger V2.5` excludes recovery parachutes (`rndRecoveryOptionInstalled == false`), enforces a strictly mechanical zero-wire launch rail interface (`IF-MECH-001`), and executes ASTM F3269-17 Simplex RTA strictly in software on `FlightControllerCubeOrangePlus` (`standaloneRtaBoxCount == 0`).
