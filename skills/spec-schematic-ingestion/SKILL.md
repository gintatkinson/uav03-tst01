# Schematic Ingestion & Bijective SysML v2 Model Synthesis

Use this standalone, domain-neutral workflow to ingest raw hardware/system schematic PDFs (`docs/reference/*.pdf`) cold into a canonical intermediate ledger (`schema/schematic_ledger.json`), verify complete visual and graph fidelity through sharded blind research audits, and compile a bijective SysML v2 Single Source of Truth (SSOT) model (`schema/model.sysml`, `.pipeline/schema.sysml`, and `.pipeline/schema-digest.json`) in conformance with `rules/sysml-ssot-completeness.md`.

## 1. Scope Ceiling & Root-Cause Countermeasures

### Hard Stop Boundary
- **Owned Outputs**:
  - `schema/schematic_ledger.json` (canonical multi-document hardware, protocol, state, and invariant ledger)
  - `schema/model.sysml` (bijective SysML v2 architectural model)
  - `.pipeline/schema.sysml` and `.pipeline/schema-digest.json` (compiled AST outputs generated by `./target/release/ingest-sysml` and `./target/release/compile-sysml --compile`)
- **Out of Scope (Hard Stop at Compiled SysML Model)**: This skill stops once `schema/model.sysml` compiles cleanly (`exit 0`) and passes Blind Audit #2 with `0` discrepancies. Do NOT regenerate or modify downstream prose specifications (`docs/conops/`, `docs/interfaces/`, `docs/epics/`, `docs/features/`, `docs/user-stories/`, `docs/use-cases/`, `docs/safety/`, `docs/research/`, `docs/management/`, `docs/architecture/`), and do NOT invoke `--forward-sync`, `--stpa-transpile`, `assemble-conops`, or `reconcile-backlog`.

### Structural Failure Modes Eliminated
| Failure ID | Unstructured Modeling Failure Mode | Structural Countermeasure Enforced Here |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `FM-01` | **Spatial Enclosure Loss**: Modeling from prose tables flattens dashed/grouped sub-assembly boxes into a flat peer list. | Visual top-down `enclosures[]` tree `T_E` extracted before leaf parts/wires; Law `L1` enforces `1:1` composite `part def` nesting. |
| `FM-02` | **Chimera & Duplicate Classifiers**: Separate boxes are merged into synthetic bucket classes, or `k` identical units duplicate `part def`s. | Strict split between `component_types[]` (`V_T`) and `component_instances[]` (`V_I`) with typing map `tau: V_I -> V_T` (Law `L2`). |
| `FM-03` | **Dropped Internal & Supply Rails**: Power/data rails inside stacked assemblies or through splitter PCBs are omitted. | Mandatory `requires_electrical_power` + `power_feed_mode` declarations and Law `L6` supply-graph reachability back to `energy_storage`. |
| `FM-04` | **Topology & Port-Domain Corruption**: Series daisy-chains flatten into stars; unrelated domains multiplex onto hybrid ports; ports sit unused. | Explicit `topology` (`series_daisy_hop` + contiguous `series_hop_index`), single-domain ports, bulkhead delegation (`L3`, `L4`, `L5`). |
| `FM-05` | **Legend, Protocol & Cross-PDF Drift**: Sidebar rules, opcodes/bitfields, state guards, and multi-PDF conflicts require costly re-reads or vanish. | Single-pass capture of `co_packaged_functions[]`, `protocol_and_register_contracts[]`, `state_machines_and_procedures[]`, `functional_invariants[]`, and `cross_document_reconciliations[]` lowered under Laws `L7` and `L8`. |
| `FM-06` | **Multimodal PDF & Reply Compaction**: Binary PDF reads render all pages on every call; verbose replies overflow coordinator context. | Multimodal PDF Anti-Compaction & Single-Pass Sharding Protocol (Section 3) + sharded Blind Audit #1 + text-only Blind Audit #2. |

   - Concrete M1 component names, ratings, signals, protocols, and constraints enter `schema/schematic_ledger.json` and `schema/model.sysml` exclusively from the input PDFs during execution.
2. **Check 23 Factual Grounding & Numeric Provenance (`factual-grounding-numeric-drift`)**:

   - Overwrite `schema/model.sysml` wholesale via `write_file` (`Overwrite: true`) without reading the prior file. Read `.pipeline/schema.sysml` and `.pipeline/schema-digest.json` ONLY after fresh compilation exits `0` and overwrites them.

## 3. Multimodal PDF Anti-Compaction & Single-Pass Sharding Protocol

1. **Binary PDF `read_file` Physics**:
   - Invoking `read_file` on any `.pdf` ignores `StartLine` and `EndLine` parameters and attaches rendered page images plus OCR for **every** page of that PDF on every call. Redundant or parallel PDF reads immediately bloat context and trigger compaction failures.
2. **Zero-PDF Coordinator**:
   - The coordinator MUST NEVER call `read_file` on any `.pdf` file.
   - Inspect inputs strictly via directory metadata (`ls -la docs/reference`) to catalog paths and byte sizes (`file_size_bytes`) for scheduling.
3. **1-PDF-Per-Subagent & Single Exhaustive Whole-Read**:
   - Every PDF-reading subagent (extraction or Audit #1 shard) receives **at most 1 `.pdf` path**.
   - It calls `read_file` **at most once** on that `.pdf` with **zero `StartLine` and zero `EndLine` arguments**, and MUST NEVER issue parallel `read_file` calls on PDFs.
   - **Solo Lock (`> 10 MB`)**: Any `.pdf` larger than `10 MB` MUST execute in a **solo subagent** (`1` active subagent, `0` concurrent peers) and be reclaimed immediately upon finishing. PDFs `<= 10 MB` run at most `<= 2` concurrent subagents.
   - **Single-Pass Completeness**: In that single read, the extractor MUST capture **both** structural topology (`enclosures`, `component_types`, `component_instances`, `wires`, `unconnected_or_spare_pins`) **and** all parametric, behavioral, protocol, and safety artifacts (`parametric_attributes`, `co_packaged_functions`, `protocol_and_register_contracts`, `state_machines_and_procedures`, `functional_invariants`, `cross_document_reconciliations`) across every inspected page (`inspected_pages[]`) so zero second PDF reads are required.
4. **Anchor-First Ordering & Resolved Scratch-Sharded Extraction**:
   - **Primary Block Diagram First**: Dispatch the primary system block-diagram / top-level interconnect PDF first so its canonical `enclosure_id`, `type_id`, `instance_id`, `canonical_part_def`, and `canonical_instance_name` symbols anchor all subsequent manual, CAD, pinout, and ICD shards. Pass the completed anchor shard (text JSON, `0` extra PDFs) as read-only reference to subsequent PDF extractors.
   - **Resolved Scratch Output Path**: Instruct each extractor to write one validated `$defs.document_shard` JSON file to `<appDataDir>/brain/<conversation-id>/scratch/shards/<doc_id>.json`. The coordinator MUST expand `<appDataDir>` and `<conversation-id>` to literal resolved paths in dispatch prompts (never passing unexpanded placeholder tokens). If subagent sandboxing restricts external writes, use a gitignored workspace fallback `.pipeline/diagnostics/shards/<doc_id>.json` and delete all fallback shards before final commit.
5. **Text-Only Merge (`0` PDF Reads) & Bounded Subagent Replies**:
   - Dispatch **1 text-only (`0`-PDF)** merge subagent reading all scratch shard JSON files + `resources/schematic_ledger_schema.json` to synthesize unified `schema/schematic_ledger.json`, resolving cross-document discrepancies in `cross_document_reconciliations[]`.
   - Every subagent (`extraction`, `merge`, `synthesis`, `research` audit) MUST keep its completion reply **`<= 250` words** (delta, count, or violation summary only; full payloads go to disk) and MUST be reclaimed immediately after completion.

## 4. Canonical Schematic Ledger Contract

`schema/schematic_ledger.json` MUST validate strictly against [`resources/schematic_ledger_schema.json`](resources/schematic_ledger_schema.json) (`Draft 2020-12`, `"additionalProperties": false` on every object) and contain all 11 root properties:

1. `schema_version` -- integer `const: 1`.
2. `source_documents[]` (`minItems: 1`) -- `doc_id`, `relative_path`, `file_size_bytes`, `page_count`, `inspected_pages[]`.
3. `enclosures[]` (`minItems: 1`) -- top-down parent-before-child tree `T_E` (`enclosure_id`, `canonical_part_def`, `canonical_instance_name`, `oem_exact_label`, `parent_enclosure_id`, `boundary_placement`, `cardinality`, `populated_in_production`, `source_ref`).
4. `component_types[]` (`minItems: 1`) -- distinct hardware/software classifiers `V_T` (`type_id`, `canonical_part_def`, `oem_model_or_label`, closed `functional_category`, `co_packaged_functions[]`, `requires_electrical_power`, `power_feed_mode`, single-domain `declared_ports[]`, typed `parametric_attributes[]`, `source_ref`).
5. `component_instances[]` (`minItems: 1`) -- physical diagram symbols / field units `V_I` (`instance_id`, `canonical_instance_name`, `type_id` FK, `oem_instance_label`, `parent_enclosure_id` FK, `populated_in_production`, `instance_attributes[]`, `source_ref`).
6. `wires[]` (`minItems: 1`) -- complete physical/logical edge list `E_W` (`wire_id`, `oem_wire_label`, closed `domain`, `topology`, `direction`, `from_endpoint`, `to_endpoint`, `series_chain_id`, `series_hop_index`, electrical/mechanical metadata, `active_in_production`, `source_ref`).
7. `unconnected_or_spare_pins[]` -- `instance_id`, `connector_id`, `pin_designator`, `signal_name`, `disposition` (`const: "documented_spare_unconnected"`), `source_ref`.
8. `protocol_and_register_contracts[]` -- `contract_id`, `wire_or_interface_ref`, `framing_and_timing`, `checksum_or_integrity`, `commands_and_bitfields[]`, `source_ref`.
9. `state_machines_and_procedures[]` -- `fsm_id`, `owner_enclosure_or_type_id`, `states[]`, `transitions[]` (`{ from_state, trigger_and_guard, to_state }`), `source_ref`.
10. `functional_invariants[]` -- `invariant_id`, `subject_id`, closed `category`, `formal_statement`, `sysml_realization_kind`, `source_ref`.
11. `cross_document_reconciliations[]` -- `gap_id`, `summary`, `source_a`, `source_b`, `authoritative_disposition`.

## 5. Structural Lowering Laws (`L1..L8`)

Every element of `schema/schematic_ledger.json` lowers bijectively into `schema/model.sysml` using the domain-neutral surface syntax documented in [`references/sysml_v2_compiler_grammar.md`](references/sysml_v2_compiler_grammar.md):

- **Law `L1` (Enclosure Tree Isomorphism)**:
  - Every entry in `enclosures[]` lowers `1:1` to a composite `part def` instantiated at its exact `parent_enclosure_id` nesting depth (`0` omitted, merged, or flattened dashed/grouped boxes).
  - Optional `0..1` assemblies with `populated_in_production == false` MUST remain modeled structurally in the AST alongside explicit production-inhibit `constraint def` / `requirement def` nodes rather than being dropped or reduced to bare booleans.
- **Law `L2` (Classifier vs. Instance Separation -- Zero Chimeras, Zero Duplicate Types)**:
  - Enforce exact bijection: `|component_types[]| == |leaf part defs|` and `|component_instances[]| == |leaf part usages|`.
  - Two physically distinct OEM boxes may NEVER be combined into a synthetic chimera `part def`. Conversely, `k` identical physical units (`k > 1`) MUST share **one** `part def` and instantiate **`k`** distinct `part` usages inside their respective composite enclosures.
- **Law `L3` (Single-Domain Directional Ports & Zero Orphan Definitions)**:
  - Every port declares **exactly one** physical or logical `domain` and faithful `in` / `out` / `inout` directionality (`0` hybrid ports multiplexing separate physical cables or unrelated bus domains).
  - Every declared `port def`, `item def`, `part def`, `action def`, `state def`, `constraint def`, `requirement def`, `use case def`, `test case def`, `capability def`, and `interaction def` in `schema/model.sysml` MUST carry `>= 1` active usage, connection, or `satisfy` / `verify` / `subject` binding (`0` dead declarations).
- **Law `L4` (Intra-Enclosure & Bulkhead Port Connection Conservation)**:
  - Preserve `1:1` wire count and hierarchy from `wires[]`.
  - Intra-enclosure wires connect peer `part` usages directly via `connection def` inside their owning composite `part def`.
  - Cross-enclosure wires delegate explicitly through typed bulkhead `port` declarations on the boundary of every crossed composite `part def` (`Subsystem.Path.port`), linking internal endpoints to their parent boundary port and chaining externally across enclosure boundaries.
- **Law `L5` (Series Daisy-Chain, Trunk & Splitter Fidelity)**:
  - Wires with `topology == "series_daisy_hop"` MUST connect strictly hop-by-hop in ascending contiguous `series_hop_index` order (`0 -> 1 -> 2 -> ...`) across distinct directional pass-through ports (e.g., `busIn` and `busThruOut`), NEVER collapsed into a hub-and-spoke star.
  - `multi_drop_trunk` and `star_stub` topologies retain their drawn branching geometry, and `splitter_distribution_pcb` / distribution nodes are modeled explicitly as intermediate fan-out `part` nodes.
- **Law `L6` (Electrical Supply-Graph Reachability)**:
  - Every `component_instance` whose `component_type` sets `requires_electrical_power == true` MUST possess an explicit connected inbound power path over `dc_power`, `ac_multiphase`, `in_band_signal_or_composite_cable`, or schema-enumerated composite power-carrying harness domains tracing all the way back to an `energy_storage` (`self_energy_source`) or external supply root.
  - Zero unpowered active controllers, regulators, switches, transceivers, actuators, or measurement modules -- including modules stacked inside nested sub-assemblies.
- **Law `L7` (Formal AST Realization of Legends, Protocols, FSMs & Conflicts)**:
  - Every entry in `co_packaged_functions[]`, `protocol_and_register_contracts[]`, `state_machines_and_procedures[]`, `functional_invariants[]`, `cross_document_reconciliations[]`, and `parametric_attributes[]` MUST lower to formal typed `attribute`, `item def`, `constraint def`, `requirement def` (`satisfy` / `verify` bound), `action def`, or `state def` AST nodes -- never left stranded exclusively in `doc /* ... */` comments.
  - Record `unconnected_or_spare_pins[]` via formal typed `attribute` or `constraint def` metadata on the owning component rather than declaring unconnected orphan `port` / `port def` nodes (preserving Law `L3`).
- **Law `L8` (Root Lifecycle `state def` & Compile-Before-Audit-#2 Gate)**:
  - Overwrite `schema/model.sysml` wholesale via `write_file` (`Overwrite: true`) without reading any quarantined prior model.
  - Declare a top-level system lifecycle `state def` in the root `package` containing `>= 2` operational `state` nodes with formal `Initial` and `Terminal` transitions.
  - Before invoking Blind Audit #2 (and after EVERY subsequent edit to `schema/model.sysml`), execute BOTH compiler gates to `exit 0`:
    1. `./target/release/ingest-sysml --schema schema/model.sysml --allowed-parts <comma-separated canonical_part_def list> --negative-invariants <ledger-derived negative assertions>`
    2. `./target/release/compile-sysml --compile schema/model.sysml`
  - Only after fresh compilation succeeds (`exit 0`) and overwrites `.pipeline/schema.sysml` and `.pipeline/schema-digest.json` may Blind Audit #2 inspect `.pipeline/schema-digest.json`.

## 6. End-to-End Execution Runbook

1. **Preparation**: Coordinator runs `ls -la docs/reference` (recording byte sizes; `0` PDF reads), verifies quarantine locks, and checks `./target/release/{ingest-sysml,compile-sysml} --help` if CLI flag syntax confirmation is required.
2. **Sharded Extraction**: Extract the primary block-diagram PDF first into `<appDataDir>/brain/<conversation-id>/scratch/shards/<doc_id>.json`, then dispatch remaining PDFs (`1` PDF per subagent, single whole `read_file` with `0` `StartLine`/`EndLine`, solo for `> 10 MB`, `<= 2` concurrent for `<= 10 MB`, `<= 250`-word replies, immediate reclaim).
3. **Text-Only Merge**: Dispatch `1` text-only (`0`-PDF) subagent to validate all shards against `$defs.document_shard` and write unified `schema/schematic_ledger.json`.
4. **Blind Audit #1 (`0` Discrepancies Required)**: Execute Phase 1A (per-PDF `TypeName="research"` visual diff shards) and Phase 1B (`1` text-only `TypeName="research"` global ledger audit) per [`references/blind_schematic_audit_protocol.md`](references/blind_schematic_audit_protocol.md). Remediate any delta and re-audit affected scopes until `0` discrepancies remain.
5. **Bijective Synthesis & Compile**: Dispatch `1` text-only (`0`-PDF) synthesis subagent to write `schema/model.sysml` wholesale under Laws `L1..L8` and [`references/sysml_v2_compiler_grammar.md`](references/sysml_v2_compiler_grammar.md). Run `./target/release/ingest-sysml` and `./target/release/compile-sysml --compile schema/model.sysml` (`exit 0`).
6. **Blind Audit #2 (`0` Discrepancies Required)**: Execute text-only (`0`-PDF) post-compile `TypeName="research"` Audit #2 per [`references/blind_schematic_audit_protocol.md`](references/blind_schematic_audit_protocol.md). If discrepancies occur, fix `schema/model.sysml`, re-run both compiler binaries, and re-run Audit #2 until `0` discrepancies remain.
7. **Hygiene & Scope Lock**: Delete any fallback `.pipeline/diagnostics/shards/` files, verify zero `.DS_Store`, zero Unicode dashes, zero hardcoded workstation paths, and Check 19 compliance across skill assets, then stop cleanly at the compiled SysML v2 model.
